# TIBS Theme 1: Technology & Innovation
# TIBS主题一：技术与创新

---

**Types of Innovation and the Innovator’s Dilemma**
**创新类型与创新者的窘境**

---

**Definition**
**定义**

Clayton Christensen’s *The Innovator’s Dilemma* identifies two main types of innovation:
克莱顿·克里斯坦森的《创新者的窘境》提出了两种主要的创新类型：

- **Sustaining Innovation:**  
  Improving existing products and technologies to better serve current customers. For example, car companies keep enhancing engines and safety features for their main markets.
- **持续性创新：**  
  改进现有产品和技术，以更好地服务当前客户。例如，汽车公司不断为其主要市场提升发动机和安全性能。

- **Disruptive Innovation:**  
  Introducing simpler, lower-cost solutions that first appeal to small or overlooked markets. As the technology improves, these new entrants often reach the mainstream and can replace older leaders.
- **颠覆性创新：**  
  引入更简单、成本更低的解决方案，最初吸引小众或被忽视的市场。随着技术进步，这些新进入者通常最终进入主流市场并取代原有领先者。

---

**The Innovator’s Dilemma**
**创新者的窘境**

Large companies often fall into this trap because:
大型公司常常陷入这一陷阱，因为：

- They focus on their biggest customers and ignore emerging groups or new uses.
- 他们关注最大的客户，忽略新兴群体或新用途。
- Company structures are slow, making it hard to react quickly to change.
- 公司结构反应缓慢，难以及时应对变化。
- Most investment goes to safe, profitable improvements, not to riskier, novel ideas.
- 大部分投资用于安全且有利可图的改进，而非更有风险的新想法。
- New or small markets seem unimportant at first, but are where big changes start.
- 新兴或小众市场起初看似无关紧要，却是重大变革的起点。

---

**Examples**
**例子**

- **Hard Disks:**  
  Smaller hard disks used by early personal computers were overlooked by big firms, but eventually took over the market as technology advanced.
- **硬盘：**  
  早期个人电脑使用的小型硬盘曾被大公司忽视，但随着技术进步最终主导了市场。

- **Streaming:**  
  Netflix began with a niche market, but as streaming technology improved, it transformed the entire media industry.
- **流媒体：**  
  Netflix最初面向小众市场，随着流媒体技术的进步，它彻底改变了整个媒体行业。

---

**Overcoming the Dilemma**
**破解窘境**

*Christensen’s Tips:*
*克里斯坦森的建议：*

- Set up teams apart from the main business to explore new ideas.
- 组建与主业分离的团队来探索新想法。
- Experiment in new or niche markets to see where changes could start.
- 在新市场或小众市场进行试验，寻找变革的起点。
- Invest in long-term projects, not just those with quick returns.
- 投资于长期项目，而不仅仅是回报快的项目。
- Encourage teams to try, fail, and learn, rather than play it safe.
- 鼓励团队尝试、失败和学习，而不是一味求稳。

---

**Birkinshaw’s Four Strategies:**
**Birkinshaw的四大策略：**

- **Reframe Value:**  
  Change what you offer and which customers you target—IBM moved from hardware to IT services to stay competitive.
- **重塑价值：**  
  改变产品和目标客户——IBM从硬件转向IT服务以保持竞争力。

- **Collaborate with Disruptors:**  
  Work with start-ups or new tech leaders. Car makers, for example, partner with EV start-ups to keep up with new trends.
- **与颠覆者合作：**  
  与初创企业或新技术领导者合作。例如，汽车制造商与电动车初创企业合作以紧跟新趋势。

- **Independent Units:**  
  Create separate teams focused on new ideas. Google’s Alphabet structure lets different groups innovate without main business limits.
- **独立单元：**  
  设立专注新创意的独立团队。Google的Alphabet结构让不同小组可以不受主业限制地创新。

- **Keep Experimenting:**  
  Make constant testing and quick adjustment a habit. Amazon and Spotify regularly launch new features, listen to feedback, and adapt quickly.
- **持续试验：**  
  养成持续测试和快速调整的习惯。亚马逊和Spotify经常推出新功能，倾听反馈，并迅速调整。

---

**Critical Reflection**
**批判性反思**

Critics like Jill Lepore argue disruptive innovation theory is based on narrow examples and ignores real market variety.
像Jill Lepore这样的批评者认为，颠覆性创新理论只基于少数案例，忽视了真实市场的多样性。

Old technologies may last, and large companies can adapt more than the theory claims.
旧技术可能会持续存在，大企业的适应能力也比该理论所描述的更强。

Carlota Perez notes success needs social and financial support, not just new technology.
Carlota Perez指出，成功不仅需要新技术，还需要社会和资金的支持。

Not all sectors fit this model; regulation and slow change can limit disruption.
并非所有行业都适用该模式，法规和缓慢的变化会限制颠覆。

The theory is helpful but not a universal rule.
该理论具有参考价值，但并非普遍适用的法则。

# TIBS Theme 2: After Innovation
# TIBS主题二：创新之后

---

**Challenges of Innovation Implementation**
**创新落地的挑战**

Turning ideas into real achievements is the hardest part of innovation.
将想法变为现实成果是创新中最难的部分。

As Govindarajan and Trimble point out, the key to success is not creative ideas, but strong execution—systematic planning, resource support, and continuous progress.
正如Govindarajan和Trimble所指出的，成功的关键不是有创意的想法，而是强有力的执行力——系统规划、资源支持和持续推进。

Many companies fail because they lack good processes or cannot connect new projects with the main business.
许多公司失败是因为缺乏良好的流程，或无法将新项目与主业衔接起来。

---

**Ambidextrous Organization: Balancing Innovation and Core Business**
**双元组织：平衡创新与核心业务**

To execute innovation well, companies need “ambidextrous” structures—keeping the main business efficient and standardized, while letting separate innovation teams experiment, fail, and learn.
为了成功执行创新，公司需要“双元”结构——保持主业高效标准化的同时，让独立的创新团队进行试验、失败和学习。

- *Structural Separation*: Independent teams work on new ideas, free from main business limits.
- *结构分离*：独立团队负责新创意，不受主业限制。

- *Cultural Differences*: Innovation teams need room for risk and failure, unlike the steady main business.
- *文化差异*：创新团队需要冒险和失败的空间，与主业的稳定不同。

- *Bridging Mechanisms*: Managers and leaders link the two sides, move resources, and ensure results can be adopted by the core business.
- *桥接机制*：管理者和领导者连接两方，调配资源，并确保成果能够被主业采纳。

---

**Key Mechanisms for Execution**
**执行的关键机制**

- Take small steps, experiment, and adjust quickly.
- 采取小步快跑、试验和快速调整的方式。
- Set special budgets and resources just for innovation projects, to avoid being diluted by day-to-day business needs.
- 为创新项目单独设置预算和资源，避免被日常业务消耗。
- Evaluate innovation teams by learning and milestones, not only short-term profit.
- 用学习和里程碑来评估创新团队，而不仅仅是短期利润。
- Plan from the start how new ideas will be integrated into the main business once mature.
- 从一开始就规划新想法成熟后如何与主业融合。

---

**Cross-Departmental Collaboration and Leadership**
**跨部门协作与领导力**

Innovation requires the efforts of R&D, marketing, production, and finance.
创新需要研发、市场、生产和财务等多部门的共同努力。

Strong cross-department leadership is needed to break down barriers, align goals, and share resources.
需要强有力的跨部门领导来打破壁垒、统一目标和资源共享。

If departments fight over credit or resources, innovation can stall.
如果各部门为功劳或资源争斗，创新就可能停滞。

Govindarajan stresses that a lack of good coordination is a major reason many projects fail.
Govindarajan强调，缺乏良好协调是许多项目失败的主要原因。

---

**Innovation Diffusion: Market “Chasm”**
**创新扩散：市场“裂谷”**

Implementing an idea is only half the challenge—the other half is spreading it in the market.
把想法落地只是挑战的一半，另一半是让它在市场上扩散开来。

Rogers’ adoption model and Moore’s chasm theory show that:
Rogers的采用模型和Moore的裂谷理论表明：

- Early adopters help start growth, but if innovations don’t win the early majority, they get stuck in a “chasm.”
- 早期采用者帮助创新起步，但若不能赢得早期大众，就会卡在“裂谷”中。
- Companies must first win trust in niche markets, then scale up to the mainstream when ready.
- 公司需先赢得小众市场的信任，待时机成熟再扩展到主流市场。

---

**Critical Reflection**
**批判性反思**

- Rogers warns that not all diffusion is good: innovation bias, ignoring social impacts, and over-focusing on technology can backfire.
- Rogers警告，并非所有扩散都是好的：创新偏见、忽视社会影响和过度关注技术可能适得其反。
- Internal issues—like poor coordination or culture clash between business and innovation teams—can block commercialization, even for good ideas.
- 内部问题——如业务与创新团队之间的协作不畅或文化冲突——即使是好创意也可能无法实现商业化。

---

# Theme 3: Centralised vs Open Innovation
# 主题三：集中式创新与开放式创新

---

**Centralised Innovation**
**集中式创新**

**Advantages**
**优势**

- **Focus and Deep Breakthroughs**: Centralised innovation can focus on long-term goals and complex technologies (such as Bell Labs developing the transistor and laser).
- **聚焦与深度突破**：集中式创新能专注于长期目标和复杂技术（如贝尔实验室开发晶体管和激光）。

- **Resource Integration and Scale Effect**: The company brings together top talent and equipment internally to promote systematic innovation (such as Xerox PARC).
- **资源整合与规模效应**：公司内部集结顶尖人才和设备，推动系统性创新（如施乐PARC）。

- **High Risk Management**: The company manages major innovation risks internally, making it easier to protect intellectual property (such as Huawei 5G, NASA Moon Landing).
- **高风险管理**：公司可在内部管理重大创新风险，更易保护知识产权（如华为5G，NASA登月）。

- **Forming Unique Core Competitiveness**: Internal long-term accumulation of knowledge barriers makes it hard for outsiders to copy.
- **形成独特核心竞争力**：长期的内部知识积累形成壁垒，难以被外部复制。

**Applicable Scenarios**
**适用场景**

- Projects that need long-term investment, technological breakthroughs, and high secrecy.
- 需要长期投入、技术突破及高度保密的项目。
- Enterprises that want to achieve differentiated competition with their own resources.
- 希望用自身资源实现差异化竞争的企业。

**Limitations and Reflection**
**局限与反思**

- **High Cost**, maintaining large R&D systems is a heavy burden.
- **成本高**，维持庞大的研发体系负担沉重。
- **Slow Response**, hard to adapt quickly to market changes.
- **响应慢**，难以及时适应市场变化。
- **Risk Concentration**, failures have a big impact.
- **风险集中**，一旦失败影响巨大。
- **Easily Affected by Organisational Inertia**, weak innovation drive.
- **易受组织惰性影响**，创新动力不足。
- The historical experience of Bell Labs and others is limited by special monopoly environments, which are hard to repeat in the current market.
- 贝尔实验室等的历史经验受特殊垄断环境限制，难以在现今市场复制。

---

**Open Innovation**
**开放式创新**

**Advantages**
**优势**

- **Knowledge Sharing**: Cross-organization cooperation and open-source communities (such as Linux, joint research in biotech) speed up the flow of technology.
- **知识共享**：跨组织合作与开源社区（如Linux、生物科技联合研发）加速技术流通。
- **Cost Sharing**: Companies can get mature technology and talent from outside, lowering internal development costs (such as Google buying DeepMind).
- **成本分担**：公司可从外部获得成熟技术和人才，降低内部开发成本（如谷歌收购DeepMind）。
- **Fast Market Response**: By using external cooperation and user feedback, companies can quickly adjust innovation directions (such as crowdfunding platforms, consumer electronics user testing).
- **快速市场响应**：借助外部合作和用户反馈，企业能快速调整创新方向（如众筹平台、消费电子测试）。
- **Supported by Globalisation and Digitalisation**: Cloud computing and digital platforms (such as GitHub) provide a base for global innovation collaboration.
- **全球化与数字化支持**：云计算与数字平台（如GitHub）为全球创新协作提供基础。

**Potential Risks**
**潜在风险**

- **Challenges in Intellectual Property Protection**: Open collaboration can easily lead to core technology leaks.
- **知识产权保护难**：开放合作易导致核心技术泄露。
- **Difficulty in Integrating Results**: External innovation needs companies to be able to integrate and turn results into business value.
- **成果整合难**：外部创新需要企业有能力将成果转化为商业价值。

---

**Hybrid Model and Future Trends**
**混合模式与未来趋势**

- **Ambidextrous Innovation Model**: Companies develop both internal core R&D and external open collaboration at the same time. Tesla opens some patents, keeps core research inside, and promotes win-win in the industry outside.
- **双元创新模式**：企业同时发展内部核心研发和外部开放协作。特斯拉开放部分专利，核心研究保留内部，对外促进产业共赢。

- **Platform Ecosystems**: Innovation is becoming more platform-based (such as Amazon cloud platform). Companies build innovation ecosystems to attract external innovators while still controlling core technology.
- **平台生态系统**：创新日趋平台化（如亚马逊云平台）。公司通过创新生态吸引外部创新者，同时掌控核心技术。

- **Industry Perspective**: Pharmaceutical companies and new materials need centralised R&D to protect patents. Consumer electronics are more suitable for open innovation and quick market trial and error.
- **行业视角**：医药、新材料等需集中研发以保护专利。消费电子更适合开放创新与快速试错。

# TIBS Theme 4: Financing Innovation
# TIBS主题四：创新融资

---

**1. The Role of Government in Innovation and Return Mechanisms**
**1. 政府在创新与回报机制中的作用**

- **Active Innovation Driver**  
  Mazzucato points out that the government not only regulates but also drives major technological breakthroughs such as the internet and GPS through high-risk investment.
- **积极创新推动者**  
  Mazzucato指出，政府不仅是监管者，还通过高风险投资推动了互联网、GPS等重大技术突破。

- **Risk and Return Rebalancing**  
  Public investment is often “privatized” by private companies. She suggests that the government should make sure innovation returns benefit society through mechanisms like equity and patents.
- **风险与回报再平衡**  
  公共投资经常被私企“私有化”。她建议政府应通过股权、专利等机制让创新回报惠及社会。

- **Mission-Oriented Innovation**  
  The government should set social missions (such as green transformation) to lead industry in collaborative innovation and achieve both economic and social goals.
- **使命导向创新**  
  政府应设定社会使命（如绿色转型），引导产业协同创新，实现经济和社会目标。

---

**2. Innovation Ecosystem and Diverse Collaboration**
**2. 创新生态与多元协作**

- **Collaboration and Division of Labor**  
  Westlake and others think that the innovation ecosystem is the division of work and collaboration between government and companies. The government is good at R&D, and companies are strong at commercialization.
- **协作与分工**  
  Westlake等认为，创新生态是政府与企业间的分工协作。政府擅长研发，企业强于商业化。

- **Supplement to Open Innovation**  
  Traditional open innovation underestimates the role of government. Mazzucato emphasizes that the public sector should deeply take part in innovation networks to ensure that society gets the benefits.
- **开放创新的补充**  
  传统开放创新低估了政府作用。Mazzucato强调公共部门应深度参与创新网络，保障社会收益。

---

**3. Lean Innovation and Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**
**3. 精益创新与最小可行产品（MVP）**

- **MVP and Cycle**  
  MVP means developing the simplest prototype to quickly test market feedback, reduce innovation risk, and focus on the “build-measure-learn” cycle and rapid trial and error.
- **MVP与循环**  
  MVP指开发最简单的原型以快速测试市场反馈，降低创新风险，并专注于“构建—测量—学习”循环与快速试错。

- **Cultural Transformation**  
  Lean innovation requires companies to encourage experimentation and accept failure, demanding changes in process and culture.
- **文化变革**  
  精益创新要求企业鼓励试验、容忍失败，推动流程和文化变革。

---

**4. Corporate Innovation Practice**
**4. 企业创新实践**

- **Amazon**  
  Amazon uses small teams and MVP to incubate businesses like AWS, showing how lean innovation is used in big companies.
- **亚马逊**  
  亚马逊用小团队和MVP孵化AWS等业务，展示大企业如何运用精益创新。

- **GE “FastWorks”**  
  GE brings in lean methods to speed up industrial product development and iteration, improving market response.
- **GE“FastWorks”**  
  GE引入精益方法加快工业产品开发与迭代，提升市场响应。

- **Success Factors**  
  These include cultural change, team autonomy, data-driven approaches, and tolerance for failure.
- **成功要素**  
  包括文化变革、团队自主、数据驱动和容错。

---

**5. Blitzscaling**
**5. 闪电扩张**

- **Concept**  
  Blitzscaling means seizing the market with speed first, even if efficiency is low in the short term.
- **概念**  
  闪电扩张指优先用速度抢占市场，即使短期效率较低。

- **Growth Stages**  
  Family → Village → City → Nation, gradually expanding scale and building network effects.
- **增长阶段**  
  家庭→村庄→城市→国家，逐步扩张规模并形成网络效应。

- **Cases**  
  LinkedIn and Uber became industry leaders through speed and network effects.
- **案例**  
  LinkedIn和Uber通过速度和网络效应成为行业领袖。

- **Risks**  
  This is suitable for industries with strong network effects but comes with high organizational and financial risks.
- **风险**  
  适合强网络效应行业，但组织和财务风险高。

---

**6. Critique and Real-World Challenges**
**6. 批评与现实挑战**

- **Operational Difficulties**  
  Westlake points out that Mazzucato’s suggestions on return mechanisms are hard to carry out in practice. The government cannot always manage high-risk investment efficiently. Many ways such as taxes and funds are needed to encourage innovation.
- **操作难点**  
  Westlake指出，Mazzucato关于回报机制的建议难以实施。政府难以高效管理高风险投资，需要多种手段如税收和基金鼓励创新。

- **Social Goal Orientation**  
  Innovation policy should consider both social and economic goals, such as public interests in environment and health.
- **社会目标导向**  
  创新政策应兼顾社会和经济目标，如环境和健康的公共利益。

---

# Theme 5: Legal Matters
# 主题五：法律事项

---

# 1. Core Types of Intellectual Property (IP)
# 1. 知识产权（IP）的核心类型

---

**Patent**
**专利**

- *Definition*:  
  A patent protects new inventions, including products, technology, or methods, giving the owner exclusive rights for up to 20 years. For example, Apple patents Face ID and iPhone designs, blocking direct copies by competitors.
- *定义*：  
  专利保护新发明，包括产品、技术或方法，使所有者拥有长达20年的专有权。例如，苹果对Face ID和iPhone设计申请专利，阻止竞争对手直接仿制。

- *Advantages*:  
  Strong legal protection; helps block copying; builds market advantage; improve a company's position in the market and can attract investment; can earn money by licensing or selling the patent.
- *优点*：  
  法律保护力度强；有助于阻止抄袭；建立市场优势；提升公司市场地位并可吸引投资；通过专利授权或出售获得收入。

- *Disadvantages*:  
  Patent applications need high fees and slow to get, may delay the launch of a product; must publish full technical details; protection ends after 20 years, so secrets may become public.
- *缺点*：  
  申请专利费用高、流程慢，可能拖延产品上市；需公开全部技术细节；保护期满20年后，秘密可能公开。

---

**Copyright**
**版权**

- *Definition*:  
  Copyright automatically protects original works—like code, software, documents, images, and videos—without special registration. For example, the source code of Adobe Photoshop is protected by copyright.
- *定义*：  
  版权自动保护原创作品，如代码、软件、文档、图片和视频，无需专门注册。例如，Adobe Photoshop的源代码受版权保护。

- *Advantages*:  
  Protection does no need much process to registration, low cost and automatic; covers many types of work; lasts a long time (author’s life plus 70 years).
- *优点*：  
  保护无需繁琐注册，成本低且自动生效；涵盖多种作品类型；保护期长（作者生前加70年）。

- *Disadvantages*:  
  Only protects the way ideas are shown, not the idea or function itself; hard to prove copying; international enforcement is difficult.
- *缺点*：  
  只保护表达方式，不保护思想或功能本身；侵权举证难；国际维权难度大。

---

**Trade Secret**
**商业秘密**

- *Definition*:  
  A trade secret is confidential information, like a formula, process, or algorithm, which a company keeps secret to gain an edge. For example, the Coca-Cola recipe is a well-known trade secret.
- *定义*：  
  商业秘密是公司为获取竞争优势而保密的信息，如配方、工艺或算法。例如，可口可乐配方就是著名的商业秘密。

- *Advantages*:  
  No registration needed; protection can last forever if secrecy is kept; costs are low, need security and management.
- *优点*：  
  无需注册；只要保密可以永久保护；成本低，只需做好安全和管理。

- *Disadvantages*:  
  If leaked or reverse engineered, the secret is lost; hard to stop or punish others for misuse.
- *缺点*：  
  一旦泄露或被逆向工程就失去保护；难以阻止或惩罚他人滥用。

---

**Open Source License**
**开源许可证**

- *Definition*:  
  An open source license lets others use, change, and share technology freely. Google’s TensorFlow, for example, uses an open source license to build a big developer community.
- *定义*：  
  开源许可证允许他人自由使用、修改和分享技术。例如，Google的TensorFlow采用开源许可证，建立了庞大的开发者社区。

- *Advantages*:  
  Grows user and developer groups; can raise a company’s reputation and promote industry standards, attract potential partners.
- *优点*：  
  壮大用户和开发者群体；提升公司声誉，推动行业标准，吸引潜在合作伙伴。

- *Disadvantages*:  
  Hard to control use; profits may fall; rivals can improve or copy the technology.
- *缺点*：  
  难以控制用途；盈利可能下降；对手可改进或复制技术。

---

**Design Right (Supplement)**
**外观设计权（补充）**

- *Definition*:  
  Design rights protect the shape or look of products, such as a phone’s appearance or a software interface, for a limited time. Useful for digital products and consumer goods.
- *定义*：  
  外观设计权保护产品形状或外观，如手机外观或软件界面，保护期有限。对数字产品和消费品特别有用。

---

**IP Strategy Tips**
**知识产权策略建议**

- Use patents for core technology.
- 核心技术用专利保护。
- Use trade secrets for secret processes.
- 机密工艺用商业秘密保护。
- Use copyright for code and documents.
- 代码和文档用版权保护。
- Use open source to grow influence.
- 扩大影响可用开源。
- Use design rights for product looks.
- 产品外观用外观设计权保护。

# 2. Data Protection and AI Compliance
# 2. 数据保护与AI合规

---

**GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation, EU)**
**GDPR（欧盟通用数据保护条例）**

- *Definition*:  
  GDPR is a strict European law that protects personal data. Any business handling data from EU people must follow these rules, even if the company is not in Europe.
- *定义*：  
  GDPR是严格的欧洲法律，保护个人数据。任何处理欧盟人员数据的企业都必须遵守，即使公司不在欧洲。

- *Transparency and Consent*:  
  Companies must clearly tell people what data is collected, why, and get their consent.  
  *Example: A shopping app must let users accept or refuse cookies and explain what happens with their data.*
- *透明度与同意*：  
  企业必须清楚告知收集哪些数据、原因并取得用户同意。  
  *例如：购物应用必须让用户选择接受或拒绝cookies，并说明数据用途。*

- *Purpose Limitation*:  
  Data can only be used for the reason given at the start, not reused for other purposes without new consent.  
  *Example: If a fitness app collects your location for health tips, it cannot sell that data to advertisers unless you agree.*
- *目的限制*：  
  数据只能用于最初说明的目的，不能未经同意用于其他用途。  
  *例如：健身app收集定位用于健康建议，未经同意不能出售给广告商。*

- *Security and Breach Notification*:  
  Companies must protect data from leaks and must report any serious leak within 72 hours.
- *安全与泄露通报*：  
  企业须防止数据泄露，严重泄露须在72小时内报告。

- *User Rights*:  
  People can see, correct, delete, or move their data. They can say no to some uses, like direct ads.
- *用户权利*：  
  用户可查阅、修改、删除或转移自己的数据，并可拒绝某些用途，如直销广告。

- *Automated Decisions*:  
  If a computer system makes big choices (like loan approval), people must be told and allowed to ask for a real person to check.
- *自动化决策*：  
  若系统作出重大决定（如贷款审批），用户应被告知，并有权要求人工复核。

- *Fines*:  
  Companies can be fined up to €20 million or 4% of worldwide sales.
- *罚款*：  
  违规企业最高可被罚2000万欧元或全球营收4%。

- *Global Impact*:  
  Non-EU companies must also follow GDPR if they serve EU users.
- *全球影响*：  
  非欧盟公司如服务欧盟用户也须遵守GDPR。

---

**EU AI Act**
**欧盟人工智能法案**

- *Definition*:  
  The AI Act is the world’s first big law to directly manage artificial intelligence. It sorts AI into groups by risk.
- *定义*：  
  AI法案是全球首个直接监管人工智能的重要法律，按风险对AI分类。

- *Banned AI*:  
  Some AI uses are banned, like systems for social scoring or live facial recognition in public.
- *禁止用途*：  
  某些AI应用被禁止，如社会评分系统或公共场所实时人脸识别。

- *High-Risk AI*:  
  AI in areas like health, hiring, or policing must meet strict rules. It must use fair data, explain decisions, and keep good records.  
  *Example: If a hospital uses AI for diagnosis, doctors and patients must know how it works and can question the results.*
- *高风险AI*：  
  医疗、招聘、执法等领域的AI必须满足严格规定，采用公平数据，决策可解释且记录完备。  
  *例如：医院用AI诊断，医生和患者须了解其原理并可质疑结果。*

- *Low/Minimal-Risk AI*:  
  For simple AI tools (like spam filters or chatbots), only basic rules or simple user notice are needed.
- *低/极低风险AI*：  
  简单AI工具（如垃圾邮件过滤器、聊天机器人）只需基本规定或用户提示。

- *Extra Rules for High-Risk*:  
  Must keep technical documents and let authorities check.  
  Must let humans oversee, pause, or change decisions.  
  Must tell users when AI is used and give reasons for its choices.
- *高风险额外规定*：  
  须保留技术文件，接受监管抽查。  
  必须允许人工监督、暂停或修改决策。  
  必须告知用户何时用AI，并说明原因。

- *Fines*:  
  Breaking the rules can mean fines up to €30 million or 6% of revenue.
- *罚款*：  
  违规最高罚款可达3000万欧元或6%营收。

- *Global Scope*:  
  Any company whose AI touches EU people must follow these rules.
- *全球适用*：  
  任何AI涉及欧盟用户的公司都必须遵守该法案。

---

**Critical View**
**批判性观点**

Some say GDPR and the AI Act are too hard for small businesses to follow and too slow for fast-changing technology.
有人认为GDPR和AI法案对小企业来说太难执行，对技术快速发展也反应太慢。

Firms may focus more on paperwork than real protection, and enforcement is not always strong enough.
企业可能更关注文书工作而非实际保护，监管执法也并不总是有力。

These laws are world-leading, but many gaps and new risks remain as AI grows.
这些法律在全球领先，但AI发展中仍存在许多漏洞和新风险。

# Theme 6: Responsibility and Ethics RRI
# 主题六：责任与伦理 RRI

---

**1. The Four Main Principles of RRI and Real Cases**
**1. RRI的四项核心原则及实际案例**

- **Anticipation**  
  This encourages early identification of social, ethical, and environmental impacts in the early stages of innovation. Microsoft uses an ethics review mechanism to give early warnings about AI algorithm bias. Although it is impossible to predict everything, it can reduce the risk of technology getting out of control.
- **前瞻性**  
  鼓励在创新早期识别社会、伦理和环境影响。微软采用伦理审查机制对AI算法偏见进行预警。虽无法预测一切，但能降低技术失控风险。

- **Inclusion**  
  This stresses that many different stakeholders should join in decision-making. The EU’s biotechnology policy lets farmers, environmental groups, and consumers all have a say, which increases the legitimacy of innovation and public approval.
- **包容性**  
  强调多方利益相关者参与决策。欧盟生物技术政策让农民、环保组织、消费者等都能发声，提高创新的合法性和公众认可度。

- **Reflexivity**  
  This encourages self-examination and continuous checking of company assumptions and the results of decisions. For example, Patagonia keeps reflecting on its supply chain to push forward environmental protection and sustainability.
- **反思性**  
  鼓励企业自我审视，不断检验假设和决策结果。例如，Patagonia不断反思供应链，推进环保与可持续发展。

- **Responsiveness**  
  This requires companies to adjust their innovation path based on feedback. For example, IKEA improved its raw materials policy because of environmental pressure, making the company more sensitive to social demands.
- **响应性**  
  要求企业根据反馈调整创新路径。例如，IKEA因环保压力改进原材料政策，对社会诉求更敏感。

These principles together make sure that companies can control negative side effects when pushing innovation and put responsibility into the whole innovation process.
这些原则共同确保企业在推进创新时能控制负面影响，将责任贯穿于整个创新过程。

---

**2. The Value and Role of RRI**
**2. RRI的价值与作用**

- **Social Trust and Market Advantage**  
  By paying attention to privacy, environmental protection, and social welfare (such as Apple and Unilever), companies improve brand reputation and customer loyalty.
- **社会信任与市场优势**  
  注重隐私、环保和社会福祉（如苹果、联合利华）有助于提升品牌声誉和客户忠诚度。

- **Avoiding Risks and Leading in Policy**  
  Actively practicing RRI helps companies adapt early to new rules like data compliance (such as GDPR) and lowers the risk of being heavily fined for breaking the rules.
- **规避风险并引领政策**  
  主动践行RRI帮助企业及早适应新规（如数据合规），降低违规被重罚的风险。

- **Finding Innovation Opportunities**  
  Including more outside views helps companies find potential needs and markets earlier (such as open hardware and crowdfunding models).
- **发现创新机会**  
  引入外部观点有助于企业更早发现潜在需求和市场（如开源硬件、众筹模式）。

- **Attracting Talent and Building Team Unity**  
  New generations who agree with social responsibility are more willing to join organizations that practice RRI.
- **吸引人才和团队凝聚**  
  认同社会责任的新一代更愿意加入践行RRI的组织。

- **Promoting Algorithm Transparency and Privacy Protection**  
  In AI and data science, RRI puts special emphasis on the transparency of algorithm decisions and protection of data privacy. It pushes companies to use ethical technologies like explainable AI and differential privacy to prevent discrimination and misuse, and to increase public trust.
- **促进算法透明与隐私保护**  
  在AI与数据科学领域，RRI强调算法决策透明和数据隐私保护，推动企业采用可解释AI、差分隐私等伦理技术，防止歧视和滥用，提升公众信任。

---

**3. Challenges and Critical Reflection**
**3. 挑战与批判性反思**

- **Hard to Put into Practice, Lack of Evaluation Systems**  
  Many companies treat RRI as just a brand talk. In practice, it costs a lot and there is no effective evaluation, so it often becomes empty talk.
- **难以落地、缺乏评价体系**  
  许多企业将RRI仅当作品牌宣传。实践中成本高且无有效评价，往往流于空谈。

- **Conflict Between Ethics and Economic Goals**  
  Under market pressure, companies often find it hard to balance short-term profits and long-term responsibility, and RRI can easily be pushed aside in a tough business environment.
- **伦理与经济目标冲突**  
  在市场压力下，企业常难以平衡短期利润与长期责任，RRI易被激烈商业环境边缘化。

- **Hard to Fully Predict New Technology Risks**  
  Especially in AI and big data, uncertainty and judging social impact are very challenging, so governance tools need constant improvement.
- **新技术风险难以预测**  
  尤其是AI和大数据领域，不确定性和社会影响评估极具挑战，治理工具需不断完善。

- **RRI Limited to “Western Governance” Framework**  
  The effects of RRI are very different in different cultures and policy environments, so local adjustment and diverse participation are needed.
- **RRI受限于“西方治理”框架**  
  RRI在不同文化和政策环境下效果差异大，需本地化调整与多元参与。

# Theme 7: Technology Failure and Prevention
# 主题七：技术失误与预防

---

**Measures to Prevent “Normalization of Deviance” Inside AI Companies**
**防止AI公司内部“违规常态化”的措施**

**Introduction**
**引言**

“Normalization of deviance” means small rule-breaking or unsafe actions become normal after being ignored. Cathy O’Neil in *Weapons of Math Destruction* warns this is dangerous for AI and big data. It can cause algorithm bias, hidden models, and loss of privacy, making social injustice worse. Because risks in complex systems can’t be fully removed, prevention, learning, and social governance must continue.
“违规常态化”指小型违规或不安全行为因被忽视而变成常态。Cathy O’Neil在《数学毁灭武器》中警告，这对AI和大数据非常危险，会导致算法偏见、模型隐蔽和隐私丧失，使社会不公更严重。由于复杂系统的风险无法彻底消除，必须持续进行预防、学习和社会治理。

---

**1. Build Ethics and Compliance Culture**
**1. 构建伦理与合规文化**

AI ethics should be built into company values: transparency, fairness, privacy. For example, Microsoft requires its AI systems to be responsible to people, avoid bias, and protect privacy. O’Neil says openness and clear responsibility for algorithms are vital. Regular training helps staff notice bias and stop “black box” AI from spreading.
AI伦理应纳入公司价值观：透明、公平、隐私保护。例如，微软要求其AI系统对人负责、避免偏见、保护隐私。O’Neil认为算法的开放性和明确责任至关重要。定期培训有助于员工发现偏见，阻止“黑箱”AI扩散。

---

**2. Oversight and Accountability**
**2. 监督与问责**

Companies should use independent ethics boards to check AI design and operation, preventing bias from being missed. Google once created an outside advisory board. O’Neil says outside audits are needed so many groups can help supervise.
公司应设立独立伦理委员会审核AI设计与运行，防止偏见被遗漏。谷歌曾组建外部顾问委员会。O’Neil称需要外部审计，以便多方共同监督。

---

**3. Feedback and Improvement**
**3. 反馈与改进**

Anonymous reporting and open feedback channels let staff warn about risks. Team reviews and risk meetings help spot and fix problems early.
匿名举报和开放反馈渠道让员工能对风险预警。团队评审和风险会议有助于及早发现和修正问题。

---

**4. Technical and Process Governance**
**4. 技术与流程治理**

Tools like IBM AI Fairness 360 test AI fairness and openness. Record every model version to trace and fix bias. Openness and clarity in algorithms help remove hidden bias (O’Neil, 2016).
IBM AI Fairness 360等工具用于测试AI的公平性和开放性。记录每个模型版本以追溯并修正偏见。算法公开透明有助于消除隐藏偏见（O’Neil，2016）。

---

**5. Diversity and Collaboration**
**5. 多元化与协作**

Teams should include people with different genders, races, and backgrounds to lower hidden bias. For example, Facebook’s diversity hiring aims to reduce algorithm risk. Adding ethicists, sociologists, and non-technical staff gives more viewpoints.
团队应包括不同性别、种族和背景成员以减少隐藏偏见。例如，Facebook推行多元化招聘以降低算法风险。引入伦理学家、社会学家和非技术人员带来更多视角。

---

**6. External Audits and Industry Rules**
**6. 外部审计与行业规范**

Invite outside experts to audit AI for ethics and risks (for example, OpenAI publishes risk reports). Take part in industry standards and share with groups like IEEE to improve rules and share responsibility.
邀请外部专家对AI进行伦理和风险审计（如OpenAI发布风险报告）。参与行业标准，与IEEE等组织合作完善规则、共担责任。

---

**7. Simplifying Systems and Loose Coupling**
**7. 系统简化与松耦合**

Too much complexity leads to more accidents. Make systems simpler, split modules, and use loose coupling (like backups and buffers) to stop small problems from spreading. Using several suppliers or extra aviation paths are examples.
系统过于复杂易出事故。应简化系统、模块化、采取松耦合（如备份和缓冲区），防止小问题扩散。使用多个供应商或额外航线就是例子。

---

**8. Cultural Change and Leadership**
**8. 文化变革与领导力**

Promote a “safety first” culture and let everyone raise concerns. After NASA’s Challenger disaster, anonymous reporting was started. Leaders must set an example, respond to warnings, and support accountability.
推动“安全第一”文化，让每个人都能提出问题。NASA挑战者号事故后，实行匿名举报。领导者须以身作则，重视预警，支持问责。

---

**9. Facing Risk and Social Bias**
**9. 正视风险与社会偏见**

O’Neil notes that bias is often rooted in society and can’t be fixed by company rules alone. Even strong prevention can’t remove all risk. Each failure should be a lesson for learning, teamwork, and policy innovation for better tech governance.
O’Neil指出，偏见常根植于社会，单靠公司规则无法根除。即使强力预防也不能消除所有风险。每次失败都应成为学习、团队协作和政策创新的机会，以提升技术治理。

---

# Theme 8: Social Failure
# 主题八：社会失灵

---

**Legal and Regulatory Recommendations to Ensure Free and Fair Elections**
**确保自由公平选举的法律与监管建议**

**Introduction**
**引言**

The Cambridge Analytica case showed new risks of election interference and data abuse. Current election laws cannot deal with data manipulation and targeted ads online. As Cathy O’Neil (*Weapons of Math Destruction*) and Caroline Criado Perez (*Invisible Women*) point out, digital systems can make social bias worse, so reforms are needed at many levels.
剑桥分析案暴露了选举干预和数据滥用的新风险。现有选举法律难以应对网络数据操纵和定向广告。Cathy O’Neil（《数学毁灭武器》）和Caroline Criado Perez（《看不见的女性》）指出，数字系统会加剧社会偏见，因此多层面改革势在必行。

---

**1. Update Legal Frameworks for the Digital Age**
**1. 为数字时代更新法律框架**

- **Special Laws**  
  Make laws focused on social media and data control. Political ads must clearly show their data sources and targeting. Secret operations should be banned. GDPR’s data transparency can be a model. Perez says data collection standards must avoid gender and group bias to stop hidden unfairness.
- **专项立法**  
  针对社交媒体和数据管理立法。政治广告须明确公开数据来源与定向方式，禁止隐秘操作。GDPR的数据透明可为典范。Perez认为，数据采集标准须避免性别和群体偏见，防止隐性不公。

---

**2. Strengthen Platform Responsibility and Supervision**
**2. 强化平台责任与监管**

- **Platform Accountability**  
  Social platforms should have ways to spot and remove false information and manipulation. They must take social responsibility. Third-party audits (like the EU Digital Services Act) can check compliance.  
  O’Neil says platforms need to accept outside checks of their algorithms and content control, to stop social injustice.
- **平台问责**  
  社交平台应有能力识别并删除虚假信息和操控行为，承担社会责任。第三方审计（如欧盟数字服务法案）可进行合规检查。O’Neil认为，平台需接受外部审核算法和内容管理，以防止社会不公。

---

**3. Improve Digital Literacy and Transparency**
**3. 提升数字素养与透明度**

- **Digital Literacy Education**  
  Schools should teach about algorithm bias and fake news to build citizens’ skills (like in Estonia). Perez notes that algorithms often miss the needs of diverse groups and says education must build public awareness of data blind spots.
- **数字素养教育**  
  学校应教授算法偏见和假新闻，提高公民能力（如爱沙尼亚）。Perez指出，算法常忽略多元群体需求，教育应提升公众对数据盲区的认知。

- **Open Information Platforms**  
  Create official sites to show all legal political ads and data, so the public can check and access information.
- **开放信息平台**  
  建立官方平台公开所有合法政治广告及数据，方便公众核查和获取信息。

---

**4. Cross-Border Collaboration and Tech Governance**
**4. 跨境合作与技术治理**

- **International Standards**  
  Build unified data privacy and ad laws to help global cooperation (like the UN’s Paris Agreement).  
- **国际标准**  
  制定统一的数据隐私和广告法律，促进全球合作（如联合国巴黎协定）。

- **Government-Business Cooperation**  
  Platforms must work with election agencies to fight fake news and tech abuse. New tools like AI and blockchain (for example, Estonia’s blockchain voting) can raise trust and openness.
- **政企协作**  
  平台须与选举机构协作，打击假新闻与技术滥用。AI、区块链等新技术（如爱沙尼亚区块链投票）可提升信任与透明。

---

**5. Build Social Resilience and Public Response**
**5. 建立社会韧性与公众应对**

- **Resilience**  
  Besides laws and platform rules, society must learn to deal with risks and recover from harm. Teach people to question online info, report problems, and join together for better rules. Communities should not just wait for outside help—they can act, share knowledge, and support each other. Over time, this builds a society that can handle new technology challenges.
- **韧性**  
  除法律与平台规范外，社会需学会应对风险和恢复。教导公众质疑网络信息、报告问题、共同完善规则。社区不应被动等待，而应主动行动、分享知识、互助互信。久而久之，社会将具备应对新技术挑战的能力。

# Theme 9: Power from Data
# 主题九：数据的力量

---

**The Power of Data in Modern Society**
**现代社会中数据的力量**

**Introduction**
**引言**

Today, data is a main source of power for companies and governments. Shoshanna Zuboff, in *The Age of Surveillance Capitalism*, explains how platforms like Google and Facebook use free services to collect huge user data, predicting behavior and selling ads. This “surveillance capitalism” lets a few tech giants control what information people see and choose.
如今，数据是企业和政府的重要权力来源。Shoshanna Zuboff在《监控资本主义时代》解释了谷歌、Facebook等平台如何通过免费服务收集大量用户数据，用于预测行为和广告销售。这种“监控资本主义”让少数科技巨头控制了人们看到和选择的信息。

---

**1. Data Monopoly and Surveillance Capitalism**
**1. 数据垄断与监控资本主义**

- **Data Monopoly**  
  Big platforms use data and algorithms to shape habits, target ads, and influence choices.
- **数据垄断**  
  大型平台利用数据和算法塑造习惯、定向广告、影响选择。

- **Power Gap**  
  Users have little control over their own data. A few firms hold most power, creating a “digital oligarchy” that chooses what people read or do.
- **权力差距**  
  用户对自己数据几乎无权，少数企业掌控大部分权力，形成“数字寡头”主导人们阅读和行为。

- **Privacy and Risk**  
  Large data collection weakens privacy and can create “information bubbles,” as seen in the Cambridge Analytica and PRISM cases.
- **隐私与风险**  
  大规模数据收集削弱隐私，易形成“信息茧房”，如剑桥分析案、PRISM项目所示。

---

**2. State Surveillance and Regulation**
**2. 国家监控与监管**

- **State Use of Big Data**  
  Governments use big data and AI for social management, such as China’s social credit system or US surveillance.
- **国家大数据应用**  
  政府利用大数据和AI进行社会管理，如中国的社会信用体系、美国的监控项目。

- **Blurring Lines**  
  Cooperation between governments and platforms makes it hard to tell public safety from control, raising privacy and ethics issues.
- **界限模糊**  
  政府与平台合作使公共安全与社会控制难以区分，带来隐私与伦理问题。

- **Resistance**  
  Civil groups and media resist data abuse through legal action and public pressure.
- **抵抗**  
  民间组织与媒体通过法律和舆论反对数据滥用。

---

**3. Social Value and Risks**
**3. 社会价值与风险**

- **Positive Uses**  
  Data can help society. For example, Amazon speeds up logistics with data, and medical data can help predict diseases. Smart cities and public health also benefit.
- **积极应用**  
  数据有助于社会，如亚马逊用数据加速物流，医疗数据可预测疾病。智慧城市和公共卫生同样受益。

- **Legal Protection**  
  Laws like GDPR give people more rights over their data. However, the good of data depends on fair rules and careful system design.
- **法律保护**  
  GDPR等法律赋予个人更多数据权利。但数据造福社会需依赖公正规则和审慎系统设计。

---

**4. Ethical Problems and Exclusion**
**4. 伦理问题与排斥现象**

- **Imbalance and Exclusion**  
  Big companies can crowd out smaller firms and regular people, leading to unfairness.
- **不平衡与排斥**  
  大企业可能挤压中小企业和普通人，导致不公平。

- **Trust Issues**  
  Data misuse weakens trust. Even with rules, the public may not believe companies act fairly.
- **信任危机**  
  数据滥用削弱信任。即便有规则，公众仍可能质疑企业的公正性。

- **Security vs. Freedom**  
  Security-based monitoring can reduce freedom, leading to protests and calls for new laws.
- **安全与自由冲突**  
  以安全为由的监控可能减少自由，引发抗议和立法诉求。

---

**5. Solutions and Future**
**5. 解决方案与未来展望**

- **Stronger Laws**  
  Countries are making better privacy and anti-monopoly laws to control data.
- **更强法律**  
  各国正在制定更严隐私和反垄断法律监管数据。

- **Education**  
  Teaching digital risks helps people protect their data and demand fair use.
- **教育**  
  加强数字风险教育，帮助公众保护数据并争取公平使用。

- **Global Rules**  
  Since data crosses borders, the world needs global cooperation for fair standards.
- **全球规则**  
  数据无国界，全球需合作制定公平标准。

---

**Critical Reflection**
**批判性反思**

Data power can bring progress, but it can also cause unfairness and threaten rights. Real change needs strong laws, public action, fair technology, and world cooperation—so data helps all, not just a few.
数据的力量可推动进步，也能带来不公与权利威胁。真正的变革需强有力的法律、公众行动、公平技术与全球协作，确保数据造福所有人，而非少数群体。
